Legal form · send to the district

Notice of Intent

Effective date of notice

Send this notice to the school district within 30 days before or after the effective date. That date becomes the annual evaluation due date in future years; an additional evaluation is due within 30 days of terminating the program.

To: The Superintendent of County Schools

RE: Letter of Intent to begin a Home Education Program

, Florida

The student named above will be instructed in a home education program as described in Florida Statute 1002.41. This notice of intent to begin a Home Education Program is sent within thirty days of beginning the program, in compliance with Florida Statute 1002.41(a–b). A portfolio with a log of educational activities, titles of reading materials, and samples of work will be kept in accordance with Florida law. An evaluation will be done and documentation sent to the school district by the anniversary of this letter annually, and an evaluation will be sent with a Letter of Termination within 30 days of ending the program.

Please record this Letter of Intent as required by Florida law, and please confirm receipt in writing as required by FL DOE policy as outlined in their Home Education FAQ document. Thank you.

cc: parent’s records

Contact information for every Florida district’s home education office is kept by the FL DOE at fldoe.org → School Choice → Home Education → District Home Education Contacts. State law sets the requirements of a home education program, and districts are prohibited from adding any of their own.

Legal form · optional, after a move

Home Education Program Transfer Request

For a student previously home educated in one Florida county who has moved to another Florida county where the program continues. Send within 30 days of moving, to both the former and the new county.

Date

I, the parent of the home educated child listed below, am requesting a transfer of my child’s Home Education Program due to a change of address:

The student named above will continue in a Home Education Program in the new county, where the student now resides. This form should serve as a Notice of Intent registering the program with the Superintendent of the new county, using the same anniversary date as the original Notice of Intent. No evaluation and no notice of termination is required because of a change of residence. The Superintendent of the former county should close out the student’s home education file and confirm the next evaluation due date to both the parent and the Superintendent of the new county, by mail or electronically.

Contact information is optional, but it helps the district confirm receipt or ask a question. Submit this form to both county home education offices after moving, and keep a copy of the form and of the district’s verification for your records.

Legal form · when the program ends

Notice of Termination

Effective date of notice

Send this notice to the school district within 30 days after the date the home education program ended — whether because of a move out of state, high school graduation, enrolling in a school, or any other reason.

To: The Superintendent of County Schools

RE: Letter of Termination of a Home Education Program

, Florida

The home education program for the above-named student has ended. This notice regarding termination of the Home Education Program is sent within thirty days of ending the program, in compliance with Florida Statute 1002.41(1)(c). This notice is accompanied by a final evaluation as required by Florida law. Please record this Letter of Termination as stated in Florida law. Thank you.

cc: parent’s records

Contact information for every Florida district’s home education office is kept by the FL DOE. Schools must accept official transcripts given to them by parents under the Uniform Transfer of Credit Rule, as long as the student passes the first grading period in the school.
